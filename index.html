Questions, answered plainly

Do I need to send you photos or text first?

No. I start from what is publicly known about your place. After you say yes, you can send your real photos, your menu and any corrections.

What if I don't like the preview?

Then say so, or say nothing. You owe nothing for the preview.

Who owns the website?

You do. You buy the domain in your own name, and the website is yours to keep, with or without the monthly plan.

Why is everything by email?

I'm based in Aalborg, Denmark, so email works best for both of us. It is also clear and written down, so nothing gets lost.

How do I pay?

By invoice, in two halves: the first when you say yes, the second when you are happy with the finished site.
